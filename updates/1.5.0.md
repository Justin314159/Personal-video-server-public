# 1.5.0

How to install it: [How to update](README.md#how-to-update). Coming from 1.4.0
(or 1.3.0), the small `homelib-update.zip` is enough: 1.5.0 uses exactly the
same bundled Python packages. The better similarity model (MiniLM) is an
optional extra, installed separately with one command (see *After updating*).

## What's new

### A new "For you"

The feed used to turn your most-watched keywords into a handful of searches and
show the top 40 matches. A couple of videos on one subject could take over the
whole feed, and "home cooking" was searched on every reload because it was a
default starter topic. Now:

- **Your interests.** What you watch, like, rewatch and subscribe to is grouped
  into a handful of readable topics ("Space science", "Woodworking",
  "Music lessons"), each with a weight. Weights grow slowly: two finished videos
  give a small interest, twenty give a big one, but not ten times bigger. They
  also fade over time (the **Memory** slider), so your feed follows your taste
  as it changes.
- **No topic can take over.** Each interest gets at most 30 % of the feed (and
  at most a third of the searches). Interests are mixed through the feed instead
  of clumped together.
- **Exploration is your Exploration slider.** It looks and works as before; it
  now sets the share of the feed (and of each reload's searches) spent on topics
  *next to* your interests and topics *outside* them. 20 % = 8 of 40
  suggestions. Exploring never brings back things you said no to.
- **Better searches.** Each reload spends a fixed number of searches (14 per
  person), split between your interests by weight, with different wording each
  time ("… explained", example titles, keyword pairs), plus the latest uploads of
  channels you like (how many: the **Channel loyalty** slider).
- **Feedback that counts.** Explicit feedback counts more than watching:
  - 👍 / 👎 on every suggestion and under the player (Like / Dislike, as before);
  - **More like this / Less like this** when a video ends, in the same card as
    "Delete after watching" (one card, not two);
  - **Not interested** on every suggestion: the card disappears and similar
    videos are pushed down. Listed in Your algorithm, where you can undo it;
  - finishing a video again counts as a rewatch (a strong yes), watching most of
    one counts a little, giving up early on one counts a little against.
- **Age doesn't matter, except a little.** A great 2015 video is as good as a
  new one. Only brand-new uploads from channels you like (or channels about
  the same things) get a small lift.
- Reloading still sets aside everything you were shown. It now runs in the
  background: if it takes more than ~20 seconds the app says so and updates the
  feed when it's done. With no internet, the feed you had stays.

### Edit your interests

**Settings → Your algorithm** (or **Tune** on For you) has a new
**Your interests** section. For each interest:

- **− / +** raises or lowers its weight. Your weight always wins over the
  learned one; **Auto** goes back to learning it.
- **Pin**: always in your feed, however small.
- **Rename**, **Delete** (a deleted interest won't come back from the same
  videos; restore it under *Deleted interests*), **Block** (no videos about it at
  all).
- **Add a topic** by typing it, e.g. "Roman history". It works with no history:
  it's searched for on the next reload.
- **Blocked** lists blocked topics and channels (add or unblock). A blocked topic
  also blocks videos *about* it, not only ones with the word in the title.
- **Not interested** and **Subscriptions** lists.

Your existing boosted keywords appear as interests marked *From your keywords*
with the same weight. Muted keywords and channels are the blocked ones. Nothing
is lost; the old *Keywords & channels* view is still there below.

### Subscriptions

- **Subscribe** under the player, on a channel's page (tap a channel name on a
  suggestion or under a video), or from a suggestion's ⋯ menu.
- A new **Subs** tab shows each channel you subscribe to with its latest
  uploads. Uploads you haven't seen have a **NEW** marker; the tab icon shows how
  many.
- Channels are checked every morning and on every reload. **Only the title,
  length, date and thumbnail are stored. Nothing is downloaded** until you tap a
  video and choose Download, as anywhere else.
- New uploads (up to a week old) are always in your For you feed, on top of the
  normal suggestions, so they don't push out discovery.
- Subscribing counts as a strong "yes" for the channel and its topics.

### Both of you

- Everything above is per profile: interests, feedback, blocks and subscriptions
  are only learned from and shown to that person.
- **For you → Both of us** puts what you both like first ("You both like
  Space science"). Anything either of you blocked, marked **Not interested** or
  **Less like this** (or a video very like it) stays out of it.

## After updating

- Nothing you have to do. The database is upgraded automatically, and your
  interests are built from your history on the first reload (or the next
  morning). Tap reload on For you to see the new feed straight away.
- **Optional, recommended: the MiniLM model.** It matches by meaning ("black
  holes" ~ "how stars die") much better than the built-in model. It needs
  internet once and ~300 MB of disk; it uses about 150–250 MB of RAM while the
  app runs. On the server:
  ```bash
  sudo bash /opt/homelib/install_embeddings.sh
  ```
  It prints `model ok (384 dimensions)` and restarts the app. To go back:
  `sudo bash /opt/homelib/install_embeddings.sh --remove`. Your installed Python
  packages keep their exact versions, so the small update packages keep working.
  Check which model is in use at the bottom of **Your interests**.
- Kept from 1.4: your sliders and their values, boosts (now interests), mutes
  (now blocks), forgotten terms, history, starter topics (minus "home cooking"
  if you never changed the list), the Shorts feed, the reload button, export and
  reset. Thumbs down on a suggestion is still a dislike; "Not interested" is now
  stronger and also hides the video.
- New settings in `/etc/homelib/config.toml`, all optional, under
  `[recommender]`: `embedding_model = "auto"` (or `"builtin"` to never use
  MiniLM), `query_budget = 14` (searches per person per reload),
  `channel_checks = 30` (subscribed channels checked per reload),
  `request_delay = 1.5` (seconds between YouTube requests). The old
  `embeddings = false` line does nothing any more and can stay.
- The bottom bar has a sixth tab, **Subs**.

## Which package

**Small package works** (from 1.4.0 or 1.3.0): no bundled Python package changed.
MiniLM is not in either package (ONNX Runtime alone would add ~85 MB per
supported Python version); `install_embeddings.sh` installs it from the internet.

## Design notes

- **Two stages.** `recommender.refresh()` (the reload button via
  `POST /api/foryou/refresh`, the early-morning run via `scheduler.nightly`)
  runs in one background thread at a time: set aside what was shown →
  `subscriptions.refresh()` → `interests.rebuild()` per profile →
  `fetch_candidates()` (stage 1) → `rank()` → prune old vectors. The endpoint
  waits up to 20 s, else answers `{"running": true}`; the app polls
  `GET /api/foryou/status`.
- **Budget.** `Budget(n)` counts yt-dlp requests and sleeps `request_delay`
  ± 25 % between them: `query_budget` (14) + 1 Shorts search per profile,
  `channel_checks` (30) channel checks per refresh. Results are cached in
  `search_cache` (6 h; a reload only reuses results fetched in the same run).
  If most searches fail, the old pool is kept.
- **Query plan** (`plan_queries`): exploration = round(budget × Exploration);
  channels = (budget − exploration) × Channel loyalty × 0.5, from channels with
  weight > 0.3 that you don't subscribe to (their Videos tab, flat);
  the rest split across interests by weight with `allocate()` (cap ⌈0.34 × n⌉,
  pinned ≥ 1; whatever the caps leave goes to exploration). Phrasings come from
  `interests.query_phrases()` and rotate per reload. Exploration = topics
  adjacent to your interests in `topics.py`, then starter topics, a general
  list, and any lexicon topic, skipping anything close to an interest or to what
  you disliked.
- **Signals** (`interests.SIGNALS`, faded by the Memory half-life): finish +1,
  rewatch +1.5 (a finish ≥ 6 h after the last one), watched ≥ 50 % +0.5,
  stopped < 15 % (after 2 days) −0.4, skip −0.6, deleted unwatched −0.8,
  like +2, dislike −2, more +2.5, less −2.5, not interested −3, search +0.3,
  each of a subscribed channel's 4 latest uploads +0.5. The 1.4 `events.weight`
  values are unchanged (the keyword profile still uses them).
- **Clustering.** Positive items, strongest first, join the closest interest if
  cosine ≥ `join` (built-in 0.30, MiniLM 0.42) else start one; interests merge
  at ≥ `merge` (0.42 / 0.55); manual topics are fixed centroids. Negatives lower
  the nearest interest's evidence by half their signal. At most 12 learned
  interests; evidence < 0.25 is dropped. Weight = log(1 + evidence); a manual
  weight (−5…5) wins. Rows are matched to the previous build by member overlap
  (or centroid ≥ 0.8), so names, pins and weights survive; a deleted learned
  interest stays hidden and its videos don't re-form it. Labels: the dominant
  `topics.py` topic, else the two strongest non-generic keywords.
- **Score** (`score_candidate`): 0.5 × interest (similarity to the closest
  interest scaled 0..1 per model, × 0.6–1.0 by weight) + 0.2 × tanh(1.4 keyword
  familiarity / 3) + 0.15 × similarity to liked/more/rewatched videos + channel
  ((0.05 + 0.15 × loyalty) × tanh(channel weight / 2), +0.2 subscribed) +
  freshness (≤ 0.1 for uploads < 14 days from a subscribed / liked / similar
  channel) − 0.6 × similarity to disliked/less/not-interested videos (or
  negative-weight interests) + 0.03 × daily jitter. A candidate belongs to an
  interest only if its cosine ≥ `join` (≥ `merge` for exploration results).
- **Assembly** (`assemble`): exploration slots = round(40 × Exploration);
  interest slots by weight, capped at 30 % (`MAX_SHARE`) of them, pinned ≥ 2;
  unused slots go to exploration, and only if that runs out may interests grow
  to 50 %. Lanes are interleaved by stride scheduling. Up to 6 new subscription
  uploads (≤ 7 days, unwatched) are inserted at positions 0, 3, 6, … on top of
  the 40 and are exempt from the reload set-aside.
- **Both of us.** Each candidate is scored for each profile; score =
  0.6 × min + 0.4 × mean. If both have a matching interest it gets a `both:`
  lane weighted by the sum of both weights; one-sided matches get half their
  weight. Hard filters: either profile's blocks, either's not interested / less
  videos and anything with cosine ≥ `near` (0.62 / 0.80) to them. Guaranteed
  subscription slots only for channels you both subscribe to.
- **Embeddings** (`embeddings.py`): vectors are L2-normalised float32 BLOBs in
  `embeddings(key, model)`. Video text = title + description snippet + tags +
  the first ~60 words of the transcript when captions exist. Built-in model:
  signed feature hashing (512 dims) of words, word pairs, 4-letter pieces of long
  words, the channel and `topics.py` topics, generic title words down-weighted.
  MiniLM loads only in background jobs (requests never wait for it; they use
  cached vectors). Similarity uses numpy when present, else a sparse pure-Python
  loop. Measured here: a full refresh + rank of ~300 candidates in ~0.4 s and
  < 50 MB peak with the built-in model.
- **Storage** (all additive migrations): `embeddings`, `interests`,
  `subscriptions`, `channel_uploads` (newest 30 per channel; older rows and
  their thumbnails pruned), `channel_checks`, `upload_seen`, `search_cache`,
  `videos.description`. 1.4 → 1.5 migration (`interests.migrate`, once per
  profile, `settings profile:<id>/algo_version = 2`): `kw:` boosts → manual
  interests (removed from the stored settings so they don't count twice; the API
  still reports them under `settings.boosts`), "home cooking" dropped from an
  unchanged default starter list.
- **API.** `GET/POST /api/interests`, `PATCH/DELETE /api/interests/{id}`,
  `POST /api/interests/{id}/restore|block`; `POST /api/videos/{id}/feedback`
  (`more|less|null`); `POST /api/candidates/not-interested`,
  `DELETE /api/not-interested/{id}`; `GET/POST /api/subscriptions`,
  `DELETE /api/subscriptions/{channel_id}`, `POST /api/subscriptions/seen`,
  `GET /api/subscriptions/new-count`, `GET /api/channels/{channel_id}`;
  `GET /api/foryou/status`. `GET /api/algorithm` adds `interests`,
  `hidden_interests`, `blocked`, `not_interested`, `subscriptions`,
  `signal_strengths`, `embeddings`. `GET /api/videos/{id}` adds `feedback` and
  `subscribed`. Feed items add `slot` (interest/explore/subscription),
  `interest`, `interest_id`, `new_upload`.
- **Audit** of the 1.4 recommender (what was there, the cooking and
  "two videos took over" causes): [`AUDIT.md`](../AUDIT.md).
- **Simulation:** `python scripts/simulate_feed.py [--exploration 0.4] [-v]`
  prints the feed mix for a fake history (2 space + 20 varied videos; only 2
  space videos; plus "not interested" on cooking).
